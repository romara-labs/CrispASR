```markdown
# Validation Questions

> Questions for the developer to validate the profile's accuracy.

---

## 🟢 Confirm (Likely Correct)

These claims have strong evidence but benefit from human confirmation.

1. **[Topic]**: "Our analysis suggests you prefer [pattern/approach]. Is that accurate?"
   - Evidence: [files]
   - Confidence: 🟢

2. **[Topic]**: "[Question]"
   - Evidence: [files]
   - Confidence: 🟢

---

## 🟡 Clarify (Needs Input)

These claims have moderate evidence and need the developer's perspective.

1. **[Topic]**: "In [module], we see both [pattern A] and [pattern B]. Which reflects your preference?"
   - Evidence: [conflicting files]
   - Confidence: 🟡

2. **[Topic]**: "[Question]"
   - Evidence: [files]
   - Confidence: 🟡

---

## 🔴 Investigate (Potential Gaps)

These areas lack sufficient evidence and need the developer's input.

1. **[Topic]**: "We couldn't determine your approach to [topic]. Can you explain how you think about this?"
   - Evidence: [what was found, if anything]
   - Confidence: 🔴

2. **[Topic]**: "[Question]"
   - Evidence: [none or minimal]
   - Confidence: 🔴

---

## Instructions for the Developer

- Answer only what you're comfortable with
- "Not sure" is a valid answer — it helps us mark gaps honestly
- Corrections are more valuable than confirmations
- Feel free to add context we may have missed
```
